Ride responsibly

Safety and rental guidelines.

These are editable example guidelines. Confirm your final rental policy with your business before publishing.

Helmet use

Wear a properly fitted helmet; request one at booking.

ID verification

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID at pickup.

Security deposit

A refundable deposit may apply; confirm the amount before pickup.

Traffic rules

Follow local traffic rules and use lights when visibility is low.

Bike inspection

Check brakes, tyres and condition together before riding.

Damage

Report damage promptly; responsibility depends on agreed rental terms.

Late returns

Tell the team early if you need extra time; charges may apply.

Emergency help

Stop in a safe place and contact the rental team for assistance.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What identification is required?

Our sample policy asks for a valid government-issued photo ID at pickup. Replace this with your actual requirements.

Is a security deposit needed?

A refundable security deposit may be required. Confirm the exact amount and refund terms with the rental team before collecting your bike.

Are helmets included?

You can request helmets in the booking form. Availability and any fees need to be confirmed by the rental team.

Can I extend my rental?

Contact the rental team before your scheduled return. Extensions depend on availability and may change the total price.

What happens if the bicycle is damaged?

Stop riding if it is unsafe and contact the team promptly. Any repair responsibility should follow the agreed rental terms.

Can I cancel my booking?

Contact the team to ask about cancellation. Replace this answer with your real cancellation and refund policy.

Do you offer long-term rentals?

Longer rental requests can be discussed directly with the team. Any special rates require confirmation.
